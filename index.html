Panduan Lengkap: Simple, Compound (FANBOYS), & Complex Sentences

▼

Dalam penulisan akademik bahasa Inggris, variasi struktur kalimat sangat menentukan kejelasan argumen. Kalimat diklasifikasikan berdasarkan jumlah dan jenis klausa yang menyusunnya:

1. SIMPLE SENTENCE (Kalimat Tunggal)
Hanya memiliki 1 Independent Clause (klausa bebas lengkap dengan Subject dan Finite Verb yang dapat berdiri sendiri sebagai kalimat utuh).
• Pola: [Subject] + [Verb] (+ Object / Complement / Adverbial)
• Contoh Akademik: "The internet helps students to find information quickly."
• Contoh dengan Subjek Majemuk: "Both software engineers and data analysts tested the neural network."
2. COMPOUND SENTENCE (Kalimat Majemuk Setara)
Menggabungkan 2 atau lebih Independent Clauses yang memiliki bobot gramatikal setara. Kedua klausa dihubungkan oleh Coordinating Conjunction (FANBOYS) dan didahului oleh tanda koma.
• Rumus Wajib Tanda Koma: [Independent Clause] , [FANBOYS] [Independent Clause]
Huruf Konjungsi Fungsi Logis / Semantik Contoh Kalimat Berstandar Akademik Teknik Informatika
F For Menyatakan alasan / sebab (*because*) The autonomous drone returned to base, for its primary battery depleted below ten percent.
A And Menambahkan ide yang sejalan (*addition*) I like programming, and I also enjoy designing intuitive user interfaces.
N Nor Menyatakan penyangkalan ganda negatif The server did not crash during peak hours, nor did it corrupt any database records.
B But Menyatakan pertentangan / kontras langsung I wanted to purchase an enterprise server, but the licensing fee was too expensive.
O Or Menyajikan alternatif / pilihan Students must update their security credentials, or the system will terminate access.
Y Yet Kontras yang mengejutkan (*nevertheless*) The sorting algorithm was mathematically complex, yet it executed in under two milliseconds.
S So Menyatakan hasil / konsekuensi logis The central server crashed, so the technician rebooted the system immediately.
3. COMPLEX SENTENCE (Kalimat Majemuk Bertingkat)
Terdiri dari 1 Independent Clause + minimal 1 Dependent Clause (klausa terikat yang diawali oleh Subordinating Conjunction).
• Subordinator Umum: because, although, even though, since, while, whereas, if, unless, after, before, when, until.

Aturan Emas Tanda Koma Kalimat Kompleks:
• Pola A (Dependent Clause di Depan): WAJIB diberi tanda koma sebelum induk kalimat!
  Contoh: "Although he was nervous, he delivered an exceptional presentation."
  Contoh: "If students practice every day, they will become proficient programmers."
• Pola B (Independent Clause di Depan): TIDAK BOLEH diberi koma sebelum subordinator!
  Contoh: "She stayed at home because it was raining heavily."
4. COMPOUND-COMPLEX SENTENCE
Menggabungkan minimal 2 Independent Clauses dan minimal 1 Dependent Clause.
• Contoh: "The students were exhausted, but they continued developing the application because the competition deadline was approaching."
⚠️ 3 Kesalahan Kalimat yang Wajib Dihindari dalam Ujian:
1. Sentence Fragment: Klausa dependen berdiri sendiri tanpa induk kalimat (Contoh salah: "Because the server failed." ❌).
2. Run-on Sentence: Dua kalimat mandiri digabung tanpa konjungsi atau titik.
3. Comma Splice: Dua klausa mandiri hanya dipisahkan koma tanpa kata hubung FANBOYS (Contoh salah: "I like coding, I design websites." ❌ → Benar: "I like coding, and I design websites." ✔️).

Bobot UTS: 20 Pts (Wajib Pulpen)
📐 Standar Konstruksi Paragraf Akademik Dosen:
• Panjang Paragraf: 100 – 150 kata.
• Topic Sentence: Menyatakan Topik Spesifik + Controlling Idea yang terarah.
• Supporting Sentences: Memanfaatkan variasi struktur kalimat secara terencana:
  1. Supporting Sentence 1: Compound sentence (dengan FANBOYS)
  2. Supporting Sentence 2: Complex sentence (dengan subordinator because / although / when)
  3. Supporting Sentence 3: Simple sentence (fakta lugas)
  4. Supporting Sentence 4: Compound sentence (pengembangan implikasi)
• Concluding Sentence: Menyimpulkan hubungan antar-argumen tanpa menambah topik baru.

2. TABEL SAKTI FANBOYS (COMPOUND SENTENCES)

KataMakna LogisContoh Singkat
ForBecause (sebab)The drone fell, for its battery died.
AndAddition (dan)We tested speed, and we checked memory.
NorNegasi gandaIt did not fail, nor did it leak data.
ButContrast (tetapi)The model is small, but it is robust.
OrChoice (atau)Update the key, or the app crashes.
YetSurprising contrastThe task was hard, yet he solved it.
SoResult (akibat)Errors occurred, so we debugged.
*Rumus Compound: [Independent Clause] , [FANBOYS] [Independent Clause]

3. KOMPONEN KALIMAT (S-V-O-C-A)

• Subject (S): Pelaku/topik utama.
• Verb (V): Kata kerja utama yang berkonjugasi waktu.
• Object (O): Penerima langsung aksi kata kerja transitif.
• Complement (C): Pelengkap sifat subjek/objek.
• Adverbial (A): Keterangan waktu, tempat, cara, atau alasan.

4. ATURAN KOMA KALIMAT KOMPLEKS

• Pola 1 (Dependent di depan → PAKAI KOMA):
Although the code compiled, tests failed.
• Pola 2 (Independent di depan → TANPA KOMA):
Tests failed although the code compiled.
• Subordinator Umum: because, although, since, whereas, while, if, unless, after, before.

5. FORMULA PARAGRAF AKADEMIK (100–150 KATA)

1. Topic Sentence (TS): [Topic] + [Controlling Idea].
2. Supporting Sentences (Variasi Struktur):
• SD 1: Compound sentence (FANBOYS)
• SD 2: Complex sentence (Because / Although)
• SD 3: Simple sentence (Fakta konkret)
• SD 4: Compound sentence (Implikasi)
3. Concluding Sentence (CS): Sintesis / restatement ringkas.

6. KUNCI MEMBACA (READING STRATEGIES)

• Skimming: Baca kalimat 1 & terakhir tiap paragraf untuk ide pokok.
• Scanning: Cari angka, tanggal, atau kata kunci spesifik.
• Pronoun Reference: Telusuri mundur ke kata benda terdekat yang cocok jumlahnya.
• Contextual Clue: Tebak arti kata dari kata sinyal (unlike, such as).
